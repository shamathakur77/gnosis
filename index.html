In December of 1945, an Egyptian farmer found a sealed jar in the desert. Inside were thirteen books the Roman church had spent four centuries trying to erase.

This is a reading of those books. Slowly. Carefully. Without the cult, without the fluff.

One essay per part. A reading companion on every page.

Sophia, your guide
Where to start · What this is · What it is not

If you are new to the Gnostic texts, ask here first. Sophia will tell you where to start, what each part covers, and what is verified history versus mythology versus modern synthesis. She does not push, recruit, or talk down.

A note before you begin

This is not a system. It is not a religion. It is a careful reading of texts that were buried for fifteen centuries because they said something the institutions of the time could not allow.

Take what is true for you. Leave the rest.
